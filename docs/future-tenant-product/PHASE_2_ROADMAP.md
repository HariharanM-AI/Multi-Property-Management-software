# Future Tenant Product — Phase 2 Roadmap

This roadmap structures the development sequence for the dedicated Phase 2 Tenant Application:

| Phase 2 Milestone | Milestone Title | Key Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TENANT-001** | Mobile Auth & Resident Identity | Phone OTP login, active stay resolution, resident profile. |
| **TENANT-002** | Digital Check-In & Onboarding | Room handover checklist, rule sign-off, emergency contacts. |
| **TENANT-003** | Digital Agreement Signer | In-app e-signature, agreement review, PDF download. |
| **TENANT-004** | Digital Rent Payments | Itemized invoice breakdown, UPI / Card checkout, instant receipts. |
| **TENANT-005** | Resident Helpdesk & Maintenance | Ticket submission with camera photos, live status tracker. |
| **TENANT-006** | Gatepass & Visitor Pre-Reg | QR / Gatepass generation (`GP-`), guest arrival alerts. |
| **TENANT-007** | Resident Community Stream | Property announcements, pinned rules, discussion threads. |
| **TENANT-008** | Resident P2P Marketplace | Second-hand item listings, condition grading, reservation. |
| **TENANT-009** | Facility On-Demand Services | Housekeeping, laundry, and repair booking. |
| **TENANT-010** | Push & WhatsApp Notifications | Real-time rent due alerts, visitor arrival, and notice broadcasts. |
